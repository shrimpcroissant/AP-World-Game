# Monsoon Route

A lane-battle card game set on the Indian Ocean trade routes of the early 1400s, made in GameMaker for AP World History.

**Play it in your browser:** https://shrimpcroissant.github.io/AP-World-Game/

**Read the illustrated rulebook:** https://shrimpcroissant.github.io/AP-World-Game/rulebook.html

It's 1410. You captain a Ming treasure-fleet voyage from China to the coast of Iraq, stopping at the great trading ports of the Indian Ocean world on the way. Pirates and rivals stand between you and every port, so each stop is a battle.

## How it plays

- **The board** is two ships side by side: your crew starts at the bottom, the enemy crew at the top, with 4 lanes between you.
- **Your turn:** draw cards, spend units to place figures on your front row, then end your turn.
- **The battle:** after the enemy places its figures, every figure acts on its own: it steps forward, or attacks an enemy right in front of it.
- **Winning:** get figures to the far end of a lane to turn that lane's light green. Turn 3 of the enemy's 4 lights green before it lights all 4 of yours.
- **Figures:** Sailors, diagonal-bouncing Jumpers, lane-blasting Artillery, fast Runners, and Swordsmen that cut through two enemies at once.

## The voyage

China (tutorial) → Malacca → Mogadishu → Calicut (India) → Hormuz (Arabia) → the coast of Iraq (the final battle).

Between battles you stop at port markets to spend your coins on goods from each region, such as tin from Malacca, ivory and zebras from Mogadishu, pepper and wootz steel from Calicut, and Arabian horses, pearls and frankincense from Hormuz. Each item helps in battle.

Includes a step-by-step tutorial, three difficulty levels, and story screens for each leg of the journey.

## Built with

GameMaker (HTML5 export). The playable web build and the rulebook page (`docs/rulebook.html`) live in the `docs` folder and are served by GitHub Pages.
